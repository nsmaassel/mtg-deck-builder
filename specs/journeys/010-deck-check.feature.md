# Feature: Check a Deck Against My Collection

**Journey ID:** J-010  
**Business Value:** Deck sites tell an Arena player which cards they are missing and stop there. This journey answers the next question: what do I play instead?  
**Test Files:** `libs/deck-advisor/src/lib/{gap,substitutes,plan}.spec.ts`, `apps/api/src/routes/advisor.integration.spec.ts`, `apps/web/src/advisor/DeckCheckView.spec.tsx`  
**Tags:** `@smoke @regression`

---

```gherkin
Feature: Deck check with stand-ins for missing cards

  Background:
    Given the card index has been built
    And I have loaded a collection

  Scenario: See what I own and what the rest costs
    Given I paste a Standard decklist copied from a deck site
    When I check the deck
    Then each card shows whether I own every copy, some copies, or none
    And I see the wildcards needed to build the deck exactly, by rarity
    And basic lands never count as missing

  Scenario: Stand-ins come with their reasons
    Given the deck plays 4 copies of a rare I do not own
    When I open the stand-ins for that card
    Then I see cards from my collection that do the same job, best match first
    And each one says why it matches (shared role, mana value, card type)
    And each one says what it fails to do when it misses the point of the original
    And every stand-in is legal in the format and castable in the deck's colours

  Scenario: A cheaper craft is offered alongside owned cards
    Given a missing rare has an uncommon that does the same job
    When I open the stand-ins
    Then the uncommon is listed as a craft with the wildcard it costs

  Scenario: A version I can build today
    Given I am missing several cards and hold a few wildcards
    When the deck is checked
    Then wildcards go to the cards that are hardest to replace
    And every other missing card is swapped for the closest card I own
    And the resulting list has the same number of cards as the original
    And I can copy it in MTG Arena import format

  Scenario: Keep my wildcards
    Given the plan spends wildcards
    When I choose "Keep them, swaps only"
    Then the plan uses no wildcards and fills every gap from my collection

  Scenario: Lands are replaced by lands
    Given the deck plays a dual land I do not own
    Then its stand-ins are lands that make the same colours
    And lands that enter tapped or restrict their mana are marked down
    And basic lands are the fallback

  Scenario: Commander and Brawl decks
    Given I paste a Brawl deck with a Commander section
    Then stand-ins respect the commander's colour identity and the singleton rule
    And a missing commander is never swapped, only crafted

  Scenario: Cards that cannot be played
    Given the list contains a card that is not legal in the format, or not on Arena
    Then the card is flagged with the reason and counts as missing
    And it is replaced like any other missing card
```

## How a stand-in is scored

Code decides; nothing here is generated by a language model.

| Signal | Weight (spell) | Source |
|--------|----------------|--------|
| Role: overlap of functional tags, specific tags and primary roles weighted up | 42% | Scryfall Tagger oracle tags |
| Rules text similarity (TF-IDF) | 13% | Scryfall oracle text |
| Card type | 12% | Scryfall |
| Mana value closeness (dearer hurts more than cheaper) | 17% | Scryfall |
| Colour fit against the original | 4% | Scryfall |
| Archetype evidence: share of the archetype's lists that play it | 12% | Meta snapshot, when the deck came from the meta |

Lands are scored on the mana they make (40%, marked down when the mana is restricted or costs mana to filter), role (30%), entering untapped (15%), archetype evidence (10% when known) and rules text (5%).

A stand-in scoring 0.65 or more is a **close match**, 0.45 or more a **partial match** (0.35 when it does one of the original's primary jobs), anything lower a **stopgap**. A card that shares none of the original's primary roles is a stopgap whatever its score.

## Acceptance Criteria

| Criterion | How Tested |
|-----------|-----------|
| Owned copies go to the main deck before the sideboard | `gap.spec.ts` |
| A companion listed twice by Arena is counted once | `gap.spec.ts` |
| Deck colours ignore the off-colour half of an adventure card and stray sideboard cards | `gap.spec.ts` |
| The same spare copy is never handed to two missing cards | `plan.spec.ts` |
| Wildcards spent never exceed the inventory | `plan.spec.ts` |
| The response carries display fields only, not full card records | `advisor.integration.spec.ts` |
